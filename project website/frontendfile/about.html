❓ Frequently Asked Questions
How do I post a new research or hackathon project? ▾
Navigating to the Projects tab and clicking "+ Post New Project" allows any registered student or faculty member to publish a project title, required skills, team size, duration, and faculty mentor details.
Who can review project application submissions? ▾
Project leads and designated faculty mentors receive candidate dossiers directly in their Applications Tracker. They can inspect candidate roll numbers, statement of motivation, skills, and issue instant approvals.
Can non-computer science students apply for engineering projects? ▾
Yes! Campus Nexus actively promotes multidisciplinary collaboration across all 12 departments, including BioTech, Electrical Engineering, Mechatronics, Cyber Security, and FinTech.
How does the Kanban task board work? ▾
Every project team has an interactive Management workspace. Tasks can be moved seamlessly between "To Do", "In Progress", and "Done" columns, while tracking hardware lab resources and team member assignments.
